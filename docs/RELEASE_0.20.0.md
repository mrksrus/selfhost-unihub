# 0.20.0: Tasks from email

An email with something to do in it, a bill to pay, an article to read, a
place to remember, becomes a task with one click, and the task leads back to
the email.

### Added

- **Add task** in the mail reader and the message menu. The subject becomes
  the title, the task goes to a list of your choice (or a new one), and a day
  with a reminder puts it in the calendar.
- The task shows the email it came from, with its attachments, in ToDo,
  Today and the Calendar, and opens it with **Open email**. If the email is
  removed later, the task keeps the summary.
- The reader lists the tasks made from a message; a mail row shows a small
  task mark.
